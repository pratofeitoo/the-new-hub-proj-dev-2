# Source-to-Approved Lifecycle

> Documentation only — no files are moved by this wiki page.

## Stages

1. **01-work** — active drafting. Status `rascunho` / `em-revisao`. Source: task-linked refinements.
2. **02-review** — frozen packages awaiting gate. Read-only; changes require returning to `01-work`.
3. **03-approved** — gate-passed material. Becomes reference for downstream work.

## Gates

- **Task gate (Tarefa Executável):** definition in [Tarefa Executável](../../00-project-control/framework/Tarefa_Executavel_Definicao.md).
- **M0 / review gate:** promotion via [Fronteiras Lifecycle](../../00-project-control/framework/HUB_Framework_Fronteiras_Lifecycle.md) and [Três Camadas](../../00-project-control/framework/HUB_Framework_Desenvolvimento_Projeto_Tres_Camadas.md).

## During pilot

- Wiki writes stay under `wiki/`; lifecycle promotion is out of scope.
- Evidence and decisions record provenance but do not change lifecycle status.
