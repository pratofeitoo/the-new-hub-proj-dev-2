# Decision — Thematic Grouping of 01-work (2026-09-05)

## Decision

Group `01-work` into thematic domains under labeled subdirectories to improve navigation without changing lifecycle semantics.

## Context

`01-work` had grown with cross-cutting refinements (data, tech, platform vision) requiring clearer ownership and discovery.

## Alternatives Considered

- Keep flat `01-work` structure — simple but harder to navigate.
- Split into separate projects — heavier governance, premature.

## Consequences

- Clearer domain ownership (`dados-tech-financas`, etc.).
- No lifecycle change; existing files remain at same stage.
