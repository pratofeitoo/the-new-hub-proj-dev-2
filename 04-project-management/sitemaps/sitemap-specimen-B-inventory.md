## 4. What is and is not evidenced

**Evidenced visually:** navigation, opportunity discovery, matching, profiles, organization journey, intelligence graph, impact chains, executive outcomes, measurement/attribution, governance, Academy, and support.

**Not evidenced by these screens:** a literal e-commerce cart/checkout, a separate Classifieds workflow, a Blog product, production status, live users, or validated Business OS business results. Those must remain `not evidenced`, not `gap` and not `built`.
